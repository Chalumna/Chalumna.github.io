THE CHALLENGE

A distinctive pattern can help turn rare encounters into long-term knowledge — if individuals can be recognised reliably.

01 / RESEARCH QUESTION

Can a model learn
to recognise a coelacanth?

Coelacanths of the genus Latimeria live in deep-water habitats and remain difficult to study. Their body spot patterns offer a potential way to recognise individuals across observations, without attaching tags or handling the fish.

01
◎

Individual re-identification

Investigate whether machine-learning models can match images of the same fish using its stable spot pattern, including when viewing angles and image conditions vary.

03 / PRINCIPLES

Open science.
Responsible by design.

Making tools open is only one part of responsible research. Data must be handled with care, and conservation must take priority over convenience.

01

Open code

Code, experiments and results should be public and reproducible whenever possible. Each repository defines its own software licence.

02

Data only with permission

Images and videos remain subject to their owners' rights. They are shared only when permission and applicable conditions allow it.

03

Protect sensitive locations

Exact coelacanth observation sites are not published. Location data must not make vulnerable animals easier to find or disturb.

04

Non-invasive methods

The project focuses on observing animals without tagging, touching or otherwise disturbing them.
